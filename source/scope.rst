#############
Scope
#############

This handbook covers the following graduate programs offered by the Computer Science Department at Loyola University Chicago:

- `Master of Science in Computer Science <https://catalog.luc.edu/graduate-professional/graduate-school/arts-sciences/computer-science/computer-science-ms/>`_
- `Master of Science in Software Engineering <https://catalog.luc.edu/graduate-professional/graduate-school/arts-sciences/computer-science/software-engineering-ms/>`_
- `Master of Science in Information Technology <https://catalog.luc.edu/graduate-professional/graduate-school/arts-sciences/computer-science/information-technology-ms/>`_
- `PhD in Computer Science <https://catalog.luc.edu/graduate-professional/graduate-school/arts-sciences/computer-science/computer-science-phd/>`_

For official, authoritative program requirements, always consult the linked catalog page for your program.
This handbook provides departmental context, policies, and procedures in addition to -- not in place of -- the Graduate School catalog.
