###################
Course Enrollment
###################

The Graduate School considers full-time enrollment to be 9 credits per semester during the Fall and Spring semesters.
Students funded under research assistantships during the Summer must register for GSSM S60-001 to maintain full-time status during the Summer term.

Students are required to maintain full-time student status for Fall and Spring semesters for a variety of reasons: visa eligibility, fellowships, assistantships, financial aid, external funding agencies, and ECE satisfactory progress requirements.
See :ref:`satisfactoryprogress` for more information.

International students have strict enrollment requirements and should consult with an advisor from the International Student Services office with questions about their visa regulations.
